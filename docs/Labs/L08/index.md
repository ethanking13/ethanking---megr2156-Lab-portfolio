# Reading and Writing

Asking smart questions is important because it helps designers understand the problem and what the user needs. It allows you to come up with better ideas, avoid mistakes, and make improvements to your design. Asking questions also helps make sure the final product is useful and meets the user's needs.

# Problem Statement

### 1.1 Purpose of the Design

The purpose of this project is to design and manufacture a child-friendly toy flathead screwdriver for children ages 3–5. The screwdriver features a large, blocky design that makes it easier for young children to grip and handle. The goal is to encourage imaginative play, introduce children to basic tools, and help develop hand-eye coordination and motor skills.

The inspiration for this project came from my two younger siblings, who have several different playsets. Seeing them enjoy pretend activities inspired me to create a toy screwdriver that could be incorporated into their play.

### 1.2 How I Noticed the Problem

I noticed that children enjoy interactive toys that allow them to imitate everyday activities. This inspired me to create a toy screwdriver that gives young children another way to participate in pretend building and repair activities.

### 1.3 Ideal User

The ideal users are children between the ages of 3 and 5 who enjoy playing with building sets, tool sets, and other interactive toys. The product is intended for young children who are developing their coordination and learning through imaginative play.

### 1.4 Design Approach

My approach is to design the toy screwdriver using CAD software and then manufacture it using a 3D printer. I plan to create an oversized, blocky design that is easy for young children to grip. I also want to use a material that is strong enough to withstand regular play while being softer and more suitable for a children's toy.
