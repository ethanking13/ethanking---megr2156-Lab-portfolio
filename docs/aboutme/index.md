# About Me

I’ve always been interested in understanding how things work and figuring out how to make them better. That interest eventually led me to mechanical engineering, where I can combine problem-solving with the hands-on side of designing and building things. I’m especially interested in motorsports because it brings those ideas together. A vehicle is a system made up of hundreds of individual decisions, and small changes can have a noticeable effect on how everything works together.

I was drawn to the motorsports industry because of my personal hobbies. I owned a project car for about 5 years before I ended up selling it, and I also used to race motorcycles. Because of these hobbies it made me want to continue my love at a higher level and get a degree in mechanical engineering.

This portfolio shows some of that progression. Explore my projects to see how I approach design and problem-solving, [**view my resume**](../resume/Ethan%27s%20Resume.pdf) to see my experience and technical skills, or get in touch if you would like to connect.
